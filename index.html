Our story

“The oven is the only one in this kitchen I never had to train. It already knew everything.”
Napoli Here

A bakery kid, a sourdough starter, and one very hot oven.

Giulia Ferraro grew up above her grandfather’s bakery in the Quartieri Spagnoli, where she learned two things before she could read: dough has a mood, and you never rush it.

In 2014 she arrived with a suitcase, a bag of tipo 00 flour, and a jar of starter she still calls Nonno. She spent her first winter building our oven by hand from volcanic clay and firebrick, and opened Fiamma with twelve seats, one pizza on the menu, and a line out the door by the end of the week.

Twelve years on, the room is bigger but the rules haven’t changed. Four ingredients in the dough. Forty-eight hours to rest. Ninety seconds in the fire. Everything else is just good company.

Giulia — founder & pizzaiola

How we make it

Three things we refuse to cut corners on.

  1. 48h

    The dough

    Flour, water, sea salt, and Nonno. That’s it. A slow, cold rise gives it flavour, lightness, and that leopard-spotted crust.

  2. 485°C

    The fire

    Seasoned oak and beech, fed all night. Our hand-built dome holds heat so evenly that the base chars while the top stays tender.

  3. 90s

    The table

    Pizza waits for no one, so we don’t make it wait. Out of the oven and in front of you while the cheese is still moving.
